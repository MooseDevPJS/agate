Scope: the whole launcher shell (ui/index.html). Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: one player opening the launcher to pick a profile and press Play; occasional setup of profiles, mods, packs, shaders, skin and cape.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every profile opens on the title screen of its own Minecraft version: the game's panorama turning behind the player's own 3D skin. Refuses the category default of a grey dashboard with cards and one green accent.

OWN-WORLD: Warm obsidian ground in three tonal steps with 1px seams, warm ink, five profile colours that drive Play, focus and selection only. Archivo variable: wide and heavy for titles, normal width for UI. Real game art (panorama, skin, cape, world icons, Modrinth images) instead of decoration.

STORY: The player sees their profile as a place, knows it is ready, presses Play, watches honest progress, plays. Help text stays out of the way behind "?" tips; Italian or English.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Frameless window, custom title bar over the hero. Hero (58vh) with turning panorama, title, facts, large Play slab + folder, nametagged 3D player at right; sticky tabs below with a sliding underline.

FORM: Title screen, chosen in the redesign round (user brief: more immersive, research-led). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
